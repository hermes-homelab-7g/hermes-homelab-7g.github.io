Hermes privacy policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Who uses it

Hermes is used only by its owner, on the owner's own Google account. It has no other users.

What it accesses

  • Gmail, read-only: to read and search the owner's email. It cannot send, delete or change mail.
  • Google Calendar, read-only: to read the owner's events. It cannot create, change or delete events.

How the data is used

Email and calendar content is used only to produce summaries and answers for the owner. It is processed on the owner's home server and sent to the AI model provider (Anthropic) only as needed to generate those answers. It is not sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Hermes's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and removal

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's home server. Access can be removed at any time in the Google Account under Security, Your connections to third-party apps and services.
